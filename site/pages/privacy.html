Privacy

Your files stay on your computer.

Keeper works on your computer, not on a server. There is no Keeper account and no ads. This page lists everything that can leave your computer, and how to stop each one.

Local first

Search, text in pictures, duplicates, cleanup, notes and dictation all run on this computer. Your files are never uploaded.

Off until you turn it on

Anything that talks to the internet is listed in Settings → Privacy & permissions, with what it sends and a switch.

Secrets in the keychain

Passwords and keys are kept in the system keychain, never in a plain file.

Anonymous usage stats

Ashforde wants to know how many copies of Keeper are in use, on what kinds of computers, and which parts people use, so the next versions get better. The report is small and anonymous by design.

  • At most once a day, when your computer is idle and online, over an encrypted connection.
  • On by default, off with one switch, in the welcome tour and in Settings → Privacy.
  • See it yourself. See exactly what's sent shows the last report and the next one, field by field.
  • Reset it. Reset my anonymous ID makes a new random ID, and unsent reports are dropped.

What is sent

A random ID made on your computer (not based on your hardware or name), the day, Keeper's version and how it was installed, the system and its version, the kind of processor, rounded memory, the number of screens, your language and region setting, which parts are switched on, which main pages were opened (as rough counts), and how many crashes and restarts there were.

What is never sent

  • File names, paths or file contents
  • What you search for
  • What you ask an AI, or its answers
  • Account names, email addresses, user names or the computer's name
  • Your internet address, or a location worked out from it
  • Serial numbers, hardware IDs or network card addresses
  • Wi-Fi names, or which apps you have installed

The full list of fields, with examples, is in PRIVACY-USAGE-STATS.md.
