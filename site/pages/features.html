Files

A file manager and a library

Your folders like Finder and File Explorer, plus a library that sorts everything by kind and finds files by name, date and what is inside them.

  • Icons, list, columns or gallery; copy, cut, paste and drag, with Replace, Keep Both or Skip
  • Quick Look for pictures, video, PDFs and documents
  • Search in plain words: “screenshots with wifi password”, “pdfs from gmail last month”
  • Text in screenshots and photos, read on this computer
  • Convert pictures, video, sound and PDFs to other formats

Mail & Calendar

Every account in one place

Gmail, Outlook, iCloud, Fastmail and any other mail account, with your calendars, tasks, reminders and a planner for the week.

  • Important and Other inboxes, Send later, snooze and Remind me if no reply
  • One-click unsubscribe, rules as mail arrives, and warnings about fake senders
  • Calendars, reminders and the Planner: today, the week ahead and the Inbox
  • Alarms, timers and clocks for other places

Notes

Plain Markdown, in folders you own

Open any folder of Markdown files as a vault. Notes made by other apps work as they are, and nothing is locked into Keeper.

  • Links and backlinks, daily notes, templates and a graph
  • Notes from Apple Notes, Obsidian, OneNote and more in one search
  • Quick notes in the Island that save as you type
{{shot notes "Notes: a vault with a note in reading view, its backlinks and outgoing links" "framed"}}
The Island

A live panel at the top of the screen

It sits in the notch, or under the menu bar, and opens when you rest the pointer on it. Sizes go from Compact to Extra large.

  • Now playing with lyrics, timers, focus sessions and the next meeting with Join
  • New important mail with Archive and Mark read
  • A drop shelf for files, clipboard history, tasks and the weather
  • Cues: notes and checklists that appear when you open a given app or page

Tidy

Clean up, with nothing lost

Find what takes up room and clear it out safely. Files go to the Trash, never straight to deletion, and History can put them back.

  • Caches, old installers and forgotten downloads
  • Duplicates and look-alike photos
  • The Space Map and Large Files
  • Rules that tidy folders for you

Sync & Backup

Copies where you want them

Back up folders, keep folders the same on your devices, and mirror them to a cloud service or a server. Each one has Pause and Edit.

BackupsOn a schedule, to a disk, a server or a cloud.
Synced foldersThe same folder on your paired devices.
Cloud mirrorsYour cloud storage and servers in one place.
Privacy & Security

See who can do what

A score out of 100 and the findings most worth fixing, with a fix next to each.

  • Which apps may use the camera, microphone and screen
  • A firewall: which app connects where, with alerts for new connections
  • Network activity and your VPNs
  • New downloads checked for malware as they arrive

Devices

Your computers and phones, paired with a code

No account and no Keeper server: devices trust each other because you paired them, and every connection is encrypted between the two.

  • Send files and share the clipboard
  • See and control another of your screens, when it allows it
  • Remote Desktop and other connections from one list

AI

Any AI you choose, on your terms

Connect the AI service you like, or use one that runs on this computer. You decide what it may see and do, and it asks before it changes anything.

  • Ask about your files, and search them by meaning
  • Agents that work on your files with your approval
  • Passwords, keys and card numbers blanked before anything is sent
  • Only use AIs on this computer keeps everything here

Keep Awake

Stay on while work runs

Keep the computer from sleeping for a while, until a time, or while something is going on, with limits for the battery and heat.

  • While a download, a copy or an app is running
  • On a Mac, with the lid closed, only while plugged in
  • Works with Monorail's Stay Awake, so only one of them holds the computer awake
