Questions

Good to know.

Is it really free?

Yes. At home and at work. No ads, no account.

Do my files leave my computer?

No. Search, text in pictures and cleanup all run on it.

How big is the download?

Under 200 MB. Heavy tools come later, only if a part needs them.

Can I turn parts off?

Yes. A part that's off doesn't run at all.

Can I read the code?

Yes. It's public on GitHub, under the PolyForm Shield licence.

How do updates work?

Keeper updates itself and checks each update first.
