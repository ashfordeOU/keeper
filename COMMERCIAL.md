Keeper by Ashforde® is **free to use**, for individuals and for organisations of any size, at home and at work. You do not need to ask, register or pay to install it, use it every day, change it for yourself or roll it out inside your company.

The code is published under the [PolyForm Shield License 1.0.0](LICENSE.md). It lets you do almost anything with Keeper **except provide a product or service that competes with it**, or with another product Ashforde OÜ provides using it. Under that licence, competing includes free offerings and products on other platforms.

## When you need permission from Ashforde OÜ

Contact us before you:

- **sell, resell or rent** Keeper, a modified Keeper, or a product built mainly from Keeper's code;
- **offer Keeper as a service**: hosted, managed, remote desktop, "Keeper in the cloud", or bundled into a paid support or managed-IT package where Keeper is what you are selling;
- **publish or distribute a modified version** to people outside your own organisation, whether paid or free, if it could replace Keeper;
- **bundle Keeper** with hardware, a paid distribution or another commercial product (we usually say yes, so please ask);
- **use the names "Keeper by Ashforde®", "Ashforde®" or the Keeper logo** for anything that is not an unmodified official build (see [TRADEMARKS.md](TRADEMARKS.md));
- want terms that the PolyForm Shield licence does not give you: for example a warranty, indemnity, support commitment, or permission to embed parts of Keeper in a closed-source product.

If you are unsure whether your plan counts as competing, ask. A short written answer from us protects you.

## What you do not need permission for

- Using Keeper at home, at school, at work, in a government office or a charity.
- Installing it on all your company's computers, including from your own internal build.
- Changing the code for yourself or your organisation.
- Writing plugins, scripts or integrations that work with Keeper, as long as they do not replace it.
- Forking the repository on GitHub to send us changes.
- Writing, teaching or making videos about Keeper.

## How to ask

Email **contact@ashforde.org** with the subject line **"Keeper licence request"** and tell us:

1. your name, company and country;
2. what you want to do, in a few sentences;
3. whether it is paid or free, and roughly how many people it will reach;
4. whether you need our names or logo.
