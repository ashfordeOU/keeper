## Official builds

The only official builds of Keeper are the ones that Ashforde OÜ signs and publishes:

- on the official website, and
- on the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/ashfordeOU/keeper/releases) of `ashfordeOU/keeper`,

plus the package-manager entries that point at those same files (for example winget, Scoop, Homebrew, the AUR and Flatpak listings maintained by Ashforde OÜ). Release files are signed with the key in [`KEYS/keeper-release.asc`](KEYS/keeper-release.asc). If a download is not signed with that key, it is not an official build.

## What you may do without asking

- Say that you use Keeper, write about it, review it, teach it or make videos about it, using the name in plain text to refer to the official app.
- Link to the official website or the GitHub repository.
- Pass on an **unmodified** official build exactly as we published it, with its signature, name and logo intact, as long as you do not charge for it or suggest that Ashforde OÜ endorses you.
- Build Keeper from source for your own use, or for use inside your own organisation, and keep the name for that private build.
- Say truthfully that your own product "works with Keeper" or "imports from Keeper", as long as your product's name and logo are clearly your own.

## What needs our written permission

- Distributing a **modified** build, or any build you made yourself, under the name "Keeper", "Keeper by Ashforde" or "Ashforde", or with the Keeper logo or icon.
- Using the names or logo in the name of a product, company, domain, app-store listing, social media account or package.
- Selling, bundling or charging for Keeper, official or not.
- Any use that suggests your product, service or fork is made, approved or supported by Ashforde OÜ.
- Changing the logo or making a logo that looks confusingly similar.

## Forks must be renamed

If you share a modified version of Keeper with anyone outside your own organisation (and the licence allows it), you must:

1. give it a different name that is not confusingly similar to "Keeper" or "Ashforde";
2. replace the app icon, tray icons, installer artwork and every logo;
3. change the app ID (`io.github.ashfordeou.keeper`), the `keeper://` link scheme, the update feed and the installer and package names, so it cannot replace or be mistaken for the official app;
4. remove "by Ashforde®" from the About panel, the installer, the window titles and the package metadata;
5. keep the copyright and licence notices that the licence requires (the `Required Notice:` lines in `LICENSE.md`). Keeping those notices is not a use of our trademarks.

A fork may say, in plain text, that it is "based on Keeper by Ashforde OÜ".

## Reporting misuse

If you see a build, listing or website that uses our names or logo without permission, or that claims to be official when it is not, please tell us at **contact@ashforde.org**.

Write to **contact@ashforde.org** with who you are, what you want to do and where the name or logo will appear. See [COMMERCIAL.md](COMMERCIAL.md) for commercial licences.
